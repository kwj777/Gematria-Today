Internal Moderation Reference

Admin Reputation Scale

Private moderation thresholds for queue weighting, safeguards, and escalation decisions.

Operational bands

ReputationRisk profileDefault moderation handling
< 0High riskAuto-queue for review, tighter cooldowns, and manual approval for links/media.
0–24Early trustEducation prompts, monitor for spam bursts, standard anti-abuse checks.
25–99Baseline trustDefault rate limits and sampled moderation checks.
100–249ReliableIncrease weight of accurate flags and reduce false-positive friction.
250–499TrustedPrioritize reports and audit post edits weekly for quality drift.
500–999SeniorEligible for pilot moderation workflows with audit logging.
1000+StewardEligible for moderator nomination, policy review council invites.

Action matrix

  • Open: review within normal SLA.
  • In review: assign moderator and capture internal note.
  • Actioned: apply lock/delete/suspension and set canned response.
  • Resolved: close with reason for audit traceability.

Review cadence

  • Weekly: false-positive report analysis and assignee load balancing.
  • Monthly: score-weight tuning based on abuse clusters and quality outcomes.
  • Quarterly: threshold review with moderation burden and community health metrics.
