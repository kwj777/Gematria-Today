Tips for prompts

Type the word(s) you want calculated in natural language (quotes are optional).

For multiple targets, separate with commas or use words like "and" (example: calculate pilot and process).

For reverse gematria, give a number (example: reverse gematria 144 or what words equal 432). Range scans work too (words near 144 or words between 120 and 180).

For comparisons, use "vs" or "compare" (example: compare alpha and omega or light vs dark).
