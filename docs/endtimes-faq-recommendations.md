## Core onboarding

1. **What is EndTimes.Today?**
   EndTimes.Today is a research workspace for English gematria, biblical symbolism, and current-events pattern study. It provides calculators, explainers, and editorial resources so readers can test claims methodically.

2. **Who is this platform for?**
   The site is for curious beginners, independent researchers, and returning readers who want reproducible steps instead of isolated claims.

3. **Do I need an account to use the calculators?**
   No. Public calculator tools are available without login. A member account is only needed for account-specific features and premium research tools.

4. **Where should a beginner start?**
   Start with the Word/Phrase Calculator, then read the Gematria Guide, then compare findings with methodology-focused blog posts.

## Calculator usage and interpretation

5. **How do I calculate gematria for words and phrases?**
   Use the Word/Phrase Calculator and keep your spelling and formatting rules consistent for each comparison set.

6. **Where can I compare name values?**
   Use the Name Calculator and then cross-check related matches in gematria results and blog methodology posts.

7. **What is English gematria in plain language?**
   English gematria maps letters to numbers to examine numerical patterns in words and phrases. EndTimes.Today emphasizes consistent rules, transparent steps, and cross-checking before conclusions.

8. **How do I avoid cherry-picking results?**
   Define the hypothesis first, keep spelling and formatting rules fixed, test counterexamples, and document both matching and non-matching results.

9. **Can gematria prove doctrine or predict exact future dates?**
   No. Gematria is presented as a research and pattern-analysis method, not as standalone proof or guaranteed date prediction.

10. **Why do my totals differ from another tool?**
    Totals often differ due to different ciphers, punctuation handling, transliteration choices, or spacing/formatting rules. Match settings before comparing outputs.

## Content trust and editorial standards

11. **How does EndTimes.Today verify published claims?**
    Editorial posts should cite source material, show calculation steps, and separate observable data from interpretation.

12. **What is your editorial policy?**
    See the Editorial Policy in the site disclaimer and compare conclusions against multiple linked sources before publishing.

13. **How recent is the content on this site?**
    Key pages include a visible "last updated" date, and time-sensitive commentary should be reviewed against current events before reuse.

14. **How can I report an error or request a correction?**
    Use the Contact page with the page URL, quoted claim, and a concise correction note so the editorial team can review quickly.

## Membership and features

15. **What do premium research features include?**
    Premium features include expanded workflows for deeper lexical exploration, statistics support, and member-focused research utilities.

16. **How do I manage my account or recover access?**
    Use member login and account recovery pages. Recovery and verification steps are handled through email-based flows.

17. **Can I cancel or change my membership later?**
    Yes. Membership status and billing changes can be managed from account workflows; consult Terms for policy details.

## Practical usage

18. **What’s the best workflow for a fresh research question?**
    Frame one question, run baseline calculations, log assumptions, compare against alternative wordings, and only then draft interpretation.

19. **How should I cite EndTimes.Today in my own work?**
    Cite the specific page URL, title, and access date, and include enough methodological detail for someone else to reproduce the calculation.

20. **Do you offer financial, legal, or medical advice?**
    No. Content is for educational and research purposes and is not professional advice.

21. **Can I reuse your content on my channel or site?**
    Reuse depends on Terms and attribution requirements. When in doubt, request permission via Contact.

22. **How can I suggest a new FAQ question?**
    Send proposed wording and why it helps readers. Questions that reduce confusion for first-time users are prioritized.
