Member Forum Guide

Public Reputation Scale

Transparent score ranges help members understand progression and unlocks.

Reputation tiers

ReputationTier nameMember-facing unlocks
< 0RestrictedPosting cooldowns and manual review may apply.
0–24NewcomerBasic posting, replying, and reactions.
25–99ContributorLinks/media posting with standard limits.
100–249RegularHigher daily activity limits and content flags.
250–499TrustedCategory edit suggestions and stronger moderation signal weight.
500–999SeniorEligible for community helper workflows.
1000+StewardEligible for moderator nomination review.

How points are earned

  • +10: Thread posted.
  • +4: Reply posted.
  • +5: Helpful upvote or positive community action.
  • -10: Confirmed rules violation.
  • -25: Severe abuse event.

Fair-use notes

  • Reputation supports trust, but does not grant automatic irreversible moderation powers.
  • Admins can apply temporary safeguards when abuse or spam patterns are detected.
  • Thresholds may be reviewed to keep the forum safe and useful.
