About the seven ciphers

Each cipher links to its post in the English Gematria Cipher Atlas series.

  • English Ordinal — A=1, B=2, … Z=26.
  • Reverse Ordinal — A=26, B=25, … Z=1.
  • Full Reduction (Pythagorean) — ordinal reduced to a single digit, cycling 1..9.
  • Reverse Full Reduction — reverse ordinal reduced to a single digit.
  • Sumerian — ordinal × 6 (A=6, B=12, … Z=156). This is the cipher used elsewhere on Gematria.Today.
  • Reverse Sumerian — reverse ordinal × 6. (Shares a post with English Sumerian.)
  • Jewish / Latin — A–I = 1..9, J–R = 10..90, S–Z = 100..800.
