Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

EndTimes.Today is working to provide an experience that is usable by the broadest possible audience, including people using assistive technology.

Our approach

We aim to use semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible navigation, responsive layouts, visible labels, and sufficient contrast in core navigation and content areas. Our target is substantial alignment with WCAG 2.1 AA principles over time.

Known limitations

Some legacy pages, dynamic results, or third-party components may still need improvement. We continue reviewing forms, focus behavior, screen-reader clarity, and dynamic content announcements.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, contact us through the contact page with the page URL, a description of the issue, and the assistive technology or browser you used. Replace the placeholder contact details before launch so requests can be answered promptly.
