Forum rules

View public reputation scale

Please read and follow these rules before posting threads or replies.

  1. Be respectful. Treat members with courtesy; no harassment, slurs, or personal attacks.
  2. Stay on topic. Keep content relevant to the selected category and the discussion at hand.
  3. No spam or self-promotion. Do not flood threads, advertise products, or post repetitive links.
  4. Protect privacy. Do not share personal, private, or confidential information about yourself or others.
  5. No illegal or harmful content. Avoid threats, explicit violence, piracy instructions, malware, or fraud-related material.
  6. Use clear, honest titles. Thread titles should summarize the content and avoid clickbait.
  7. Report issues; do not escalate. Use the report button for rule-breaking instead of starting arguments.
  8. Moderation decisions apply. Admins may edit, lock, or remove content that violates these rules.
