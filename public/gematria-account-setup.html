Why a free account matters for learning

1) Preserve context

Saved history helps you continue where you left off, instead of restarting your comparisons from memory.

2) Compare faster

When prior searches are available, you can spend more time validating patterns and less time rebuilding lists.

3) Learn progressively

The beginner path works best when your earlier inputs remain accessible while you advance through each level.

What to do now

Create your free account, verify your email, and run your first few practice searches. Then continue directly to Level 1.
