Implementation Notes

Recommended RSS rendering behavior

When you wire up the feed data, render article titles, excerpts, sources, dates, and links into these sections. Keep the headline text and summaries trimmed to avoid uneven card heights, and preserve target="_blank" plus rel="noopener noreferrer" on outbound article links.

Good feed fields to include

  • Article title
  • Article description or excerpt
  • Source / feed name
  • Publish date
  • Category or tag
  • Canonical article URL
