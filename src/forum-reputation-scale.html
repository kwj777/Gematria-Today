Member Forum Guide

Public Reputation Scale

Exactly how forum reputation is counted, and what the badges beside a member's name mean.

How points are earned

Reputation comes from posting. There are two ways to earn it, and that is the whole list:

  • +10 for each thread you start.
  • +4 for each reply you post.

Points are never deducted. Rule enforcement is handled by moderators directly — hiding a thread, removing a post, issuing a warning or suspending an account — rather than by subtracting from your score.

Learning-path badges you have earned appear alongside these. See your badges for the full set.

What reputation does and doesn't do

  • Badges are recognition, not permissions. No badge unlocks moderation powers, higher posting limits, or features that other members don't have.
  • Everyone posts under the same rate limits and the same community standards, whatever their score.
  • Admins can apply temporary safeguards when abuse or spam patterns are detected, regardless of reputation.
  • Thresholds may be reviewed to keep the forum safe and useful. This page is updated when they change.
