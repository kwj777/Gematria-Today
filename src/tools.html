Difference: 336 − 324 = 12. Every letter in this cipher is a multiple of six, so the smallest non-zero gap it can produce is 6 — these two words sit two steps apart.

Per-letter breakdown

  • light: L 72 + I 54 + G 42 + H 48 + T 120 = 336
  • love: L 72 + O 90 + V 132 + E 30 = 324

Observations

  • Both share the leading L (72), so the divergence comes from their remaining letters.
  • Their digital roots (3 and 9) are both multiples of 3, putting them in the same triadic family under digital-root grouping.
  • 336 also matches words like alter and later; 324 matches voice and float — useful anchors if you want to build a theme cluster.
