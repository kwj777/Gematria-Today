Compare “light” and “love”.

GemaBot

Here’s a side-by-side using English gematria (A=6, B=12 … Z=156):

WordGematriaDigital rootLetter count
light33635

Difference: 336 − 324 = 12. The two words are only one cipher-step apart (A=6, B=12), which is the smallest non-zero gap this cipher can produce.

Per-letter breakdown

  • light: L 72 + I 54 + G 42 + H 48 + T 120 = 336
  • love: L 72 + O 90 + V 132 + E 30 = 324

Observations

  • Both share the leading L (72), so the divergence comes from their remaining letters.
  • Their digital roots (3 and 9) are both multiples of 3, putting them in the same triadic family under digital-root grouping.
  • 336 also matches words like creator and sunrise; 324 matches truth and kingdom — useful anchors if you want to build a theme cluster.
